Upload the master workbook

  1. Check — every row is validated: mandatory fields, values, duplicates inside the file and against CTDMS, and links between sheets (e.g. a customer's delivery location, a depot's companies).
  2. Preview — accepted and rejected counts per sheet, every problem with its reason. Nothing is saved yet.
  3. Import — only the valid new records are created. Codes already in CTDMS are skipped, never duplicated or overwritten. Freight rates go to the checker for approval.
  4. History & error report — every upload stays listed below; rejected rows can be downloaded as Excel, corrected and uploaded again.
