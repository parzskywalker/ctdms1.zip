How training works

  1. Press Start: CTDMS gives you a private practice copy with the v1.6 demonstration data and opens the first screen.
  2. The panel on the right explains each step, highlights the field and tells you which keys to press.
  3. You do the real work on the real screen. CTDMS checks the result in your practice copy - clicking through is not enough.
  4. Progress is saved after every step. Log out at any time; next time press Continue.

Keys: Ctrl+Enter = Next / Check · Ctrl+/ = into the panel · all CTDMS keys work as usual (F1 lists them).
